ABOUT REMINDER.PRIME

A little more space for what matters.

What it doesKeep personal, school, work, and health reminders together. Set due dates, follow what is coming up, and keep track of completed tasks.

Why it was createdTo make it easier to remember everyday commitments and spend less time worrying about what might be forgotten.
QUICK GUIDE

Make your reminders work for you.

1. Capture the taskAdd a short, specific reminder as soon as you think of it, so you don’t have to keep it in your head.

2. Choose a realistic timeSet when you want to do it, then use a subject such as School, Work, or Personal to keep things organized.

3. Check Today and All remindersUse Today to focus on what’s due now, or All reminders to search and plan ahead. Mark finished tasks complete to keep your list clear.

4. Adjust when plans changeChoose Edit to change the title, time, or subject. Snooze for a short delay, or restore the original time if you change your mind.

5. Let reminders alert youAllow browser notifications, and keep this page open for alerts. Reminders are saved only in this browser and don’t sync to other devices.

Do you really want to delete this task?

Press OK to mark this reminder as finished. The app will save the change in this browser and confirm when it succeeds.
